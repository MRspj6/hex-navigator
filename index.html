Players & roles (host only)
Cartographer edits the party map. DM can't edit the party map. Party member can only read POI descriptions (click a POI on the map). No role = view only. Only you can change roles, undo and redo.
0 · Dungeon Master Special
1 · Create grid
Creating a new grid clears the route and POIs for everyone.
1b · Line colours and 2nd grid
Grid 2 is locked to grid 1: it covers the same area, its hex size is the ratio times grid 1’s, and its rows and columns are worked out automatically and centred, so the two cannot drift apart. Navigation, route and POIs stay on grid 1.
2 · Navigate
A hexagon has 6 neighbours, so there are 6 moves: N, NE, SE, S, SW, NW. Click the buttons, or use the keys Q W E / A S D (laid out like the hexagon). Arrows: ↑ = N, ↓ = S, and hold ← or → together with ↑/↓ for the diagonals (↑+→ = NE). Each move goes from one hex centre to the next. Backspace = undo step.
3 · Route The route is kept (even after reload) until you press RESET Route. Clicking a hex moves the cursor there and starts a new segment; the old route stays. Wheel = zoom, drag = pan.
4 · Points of interest Or double-click any hex. Marked hexes show P and their list number. Click one on the map to see its description and start a route from it; click a title in the list to jump to it.
5 · Terrains
Toggle one terrain on (only one at a time), then double-click hexes to fill them. Double-click a hex that already has that terrain to clear it. With every terrain off, double-click marks a POI as usual. Terrain hexes of the same type that touch each other form one body and share one name. As the DM, this works on whichever map (DM map or Party map) is selected.
